Built around one specialty, backed by broader print capability.

Turned-edge rigid packaging is what we're known for. It's not the only thing we do, but it's the standard we hold everything else to.

Turned-Edge Rigid Packaging

This is our primary specialty: wrapping printed or specialty paper stock around rigid chipboard and turning it cleanly over every edge and corner. It's the construction behind packaging that feels substantial in hand — the kind used for boxed sets, presentation kits, and premium retail goods.

Slipcases

Book-style rigid cases for sets, kits, and multi-piece products that need a single, cohesive presentation.

Setup Boxes

Two-piece and hinged rigid boxes, sized and finished to spec — built for shelf presence and repeat handling.

Commercial Print

Alongside our rigid packaging work, we broker broader commercial print for clients who'd rather manage one relationship than several vendors: brochures and collateral, signage, packaging inserts, and general print production. It's secondary to our packaging specialty, but it's handled with the same attention to spec and finish.
