Our specialty is the corner most printers skip.

Turned-edge construction — wrapping printed or specialty stock around rigid chipboard and folding it cleanly at every corner — is what separates packaging that photographs well from packaging that photographs like the flagship product it protects. It's slower, more exacting work, and it's what we do most.

What we build

Turned-Edge Slipcases

Rigid, book-style cases with a wrapped edge for boxed sets, presentation kits, and premium retail packaging.

Setup Boxes

Two-piece and hinged rigid boxes, built to a client's exact spec for weight, finish, and shelf presence.

We also handle broader commercial print — brochures, signage, packaging inserts — as a secondary capability alongside our rigid packaging work.
